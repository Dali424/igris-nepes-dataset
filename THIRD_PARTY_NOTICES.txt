project; the source-code LICENSE does not establish a separate license for
every asset. Preserve original embedded attribution and ownership information.

Distribution changes
This package relocates runtime code and project assets, resolves references
within the package, and adds portable launch/install scripts. It excludes
recorded datasets, episode traces, videos, and development handoff documents.
